## Synthesis
- **Highest friction point:** Step 2, Home right after Add. The capture worked on the server (an "Algebra test" item was created for Friday), but Sam couldn't see that. The box cleared silently, the new item landed far below the fold in Coming up, and its pending question was only counted (9→12), never shown. The only "Algebra" thing in view was an unrelated floating draft bar. Everything after this came from that misread.
- **Highest emotional friction point:** Step 4, Home after Confirm. Sam tapped the biggest, most obvious button, and the card and bar vanished with no saved or failed message. The server had rejected the confirm with a 409 that only shows in the console. To a low-trust user, a silent disappearance after a deliberate commit feels like a crash with lost progress ("like when a game crashes and you don't know if it saved"). It's also the second silent result in a row.
- **Final decision:** Abandons at step 4, after confirming a plan and finding no "saved" message, no steps added and no clear first action. The task stopped well before plan → save → mark first step done → tell Tangent how it went. Sam never generated a plan from his own capture, never marked a step done and never reached a check-in.
- **Emotional summary:** I opened it and it was a wall of someone else's stuff, so I did the one thing that looked easy: typed my test and hit Add. The words just vanished. No "got it", nothing new at the top, so I went for the only thing that said Algebra. It had a Python step and a Sunday quiz, which made no sense for my Friday test, but the button was big so I pressed it. Then that vanished too. I scrolled once and there was my test with a little "Tangent has a question" tag and twelve questions waiting, which feels like more homework. Twice I did what it wanted and twice it ate it without saying anything. I don't know what saved and I don't know what to do first, so forget it. Paper planner.
- **Detected risks:**
  - Silent Add: low-trust users read "box cleared, nothing else changed" as failure, then go hunting and tap whatever looks related.
  - Captures land below the fold: on a 390x844 phone, a new item sorted into a long Coming up list can't be found by someone who won't scroll.
  - Pending questions show up only as a rising count (9→12, bell 6 unread). For a procrastinator who reads a growing count as debt ("more homework, not less"), that pushes him to avoid the app. Nothing on screen invites him to answer the question now.
  - Draft bar gives no sign of where a draft came from: "Draft plan · Algebra 2 Exponentials… · 5 steps" doesn't say when it was made or from which capture, so any draft with a similar name looks like the one he just asked for.
  - Confirm fails silently: a 409 on `/api/changesets/<id>/confirm` removes the card with no error, no retry and no "nothing was saved". Users will believe it saved when it didn't, or stop trusting Confirm at all.
  - Content mismatch isn't questioned: a plan with a Python step and a Sunday quiz for a Friday algebra test was confirmable in two seconds with nothing pointing out the mismatch.
  - Unexplained labels: "Goes to: Auto" added doubt at the very first tap.
- **Structural insight:** The capture → plan → done loop breaks at the joins between steps, not inside them. Each piece did something (the capture was parsed and dated, the draft card had a clear Confirm), but none of them told Sam what had just happened or what to do next. For a user who "gains trust if the first thing it does is tiny and works", feedback after each action is the product, not polish. Right now Home treats a new capture the same as the existing backlog, and treats a failed commit like a successful one. For this profile, both lose the user.
- **Fix this first:** After Add on Home, keep the result in place of the cleared box: an inline card pinned directly under the capture box saying "Added: Algebra test · Fri Nov 27", with Tangent's pending question shown inline (answer chips plus Skip) and a "Plan it" action. Leave it there until the user dismisses it or acts on it. That single change would have confirmed the capture, kept Sam away from the decoy draft, and put the planning step in front of him. Close second: any non-2xx from the changeset confirm must keep the card on screen with "Couldn't save this plan. Try again" rather than removing it.
- **Tangent-specific checks:**
  - **Context (did Tangent use what this person said?):** Partly. "algebra test friday" correctly became "Algebra test" on Fri Nov 27 (the coming Friday), and Tangent recorded a pending question instead of guessing further. That fits "ask until enough". But nothing used the context in a way Sam could see: the question was never surfaced, no plan was generated from his capture during the run, and the 5:30 PM time was never shown or confirmed to him. The draft he confirmed contained none of his context (Algebra 2 instead of his Algebra 1, a Python step, a Sunday quiz after a Friday test). From where he sat, Tangent seemed to ignore what he said.
  - **Different from generic apps (Todoist/Notion/ChatGPT):** Not shown in this run. Tangent's edge (turning a capture into a scheduled plan) never appeared for his item. He experienced a to-do box that eats text, which is worse than Todoist's instant list row or a ChatGPT reply, and he ended up going back to the paper planner.
  - **Follow-through (check-ins, round review, daily bubble):** Not reached. No step was marked done, no "how did it go" check-in happened, and the run never got to a daily bubble or round review. The follow-through signals he did see (12 questions, 6 unread on the bell) felt like obligations, not help.
  - **Trust (what is stored, can he control it; is it safe to tell Tangent things):** Not met. The profile explicitly needs to know "whether it is safe to tell Tangent things". No step reports anything on screen about what is stored or who sees it. Control signals failed too: a typed item vanished without acknowledgement and a confirmed plan vanished without saving, so he couldn't tell what Tangent had kept. The Discard option was there, but undo and status after an action weren't.

## Potential improvements

| # | Screen / moment | Actionable change | Why it matters |
|---|-----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| 1 | Home, right after Add | Replace the silent clear with a pinned inline result card under the capture box: "Added: Algebra test · Fri Nov 27", with Tangent's question inline (tap-to-answer chips, Skip, Stop) and a primary "Plan it" button. Don't let it scroll away into Coming up until the user acts or dismisses it. | It confirms the capture, keeps the next tap obvious, and stops low-trust users hunting for something that looks related. |
| 2 | Draft plan card, on Confirm failure (409 or any non-2xx on `/api/changesets/<id>/confirm`) | Keep the card visible and show "Couldn't save this plan — nothing was added. Try again." with a Retry button. On success, show "Saved · 5 steps added" plus the first step with a "Start" or "Mark done" action. | A failed commit that disappears silently is the worst trust failure for a "did it save?" user, and here it hid a real server error. |
| 3 | Floating "Draft plan · … · N steps" bar | Add where the draft came from and when it was made to the bar, e.g. "Draft from 'algebra test friday' · just now", or "from earlier" for older drafts. If the user's newest capture has its own draft or pending question, that should take the bar instead of an older draft. | Without it, any similarly named draft works as a decoy, and the user confirms a plan they don't recognise. |
| 4 | Draft plan card, before Confirm | Flag steps that conflict with the capture, e.g. "Quiz on Sun is after your Fri test" or "Python step wasn't in your note". Put the capture text at the top of the card ("You said: algebra test friday"). | A user who taps the big button in two seconds needs the mismatch shown to him, not left for him to spot. |
| 5 | Home question count and bell after a capture | Instead of bumping the global count (9→12) and the bell, show only the new capture's question in the result card from item 1. Phrase it as one optional tap ("Quick one: what's on the test? · Skip"), not as a growing list. | For a procrastinator, a rising count reads as debt and drives avoidance. One skippable question fits "ask until enough, always stoppable". |

Also worth fixing: on Home, explain "Goes to: Auto" in plain words (for example "Tangent will sort it for you") and add one line near the capture box about what Tangent keeps and who can see it. That answers the profile's required "is it safe to tell Tangent things" before the first tap.
