## Synthesis
- **Highest friction point:** Step 4, Home after placing. The planning step ended with no result. There was no draft plan, no error and no retry. A status message that sounds like internal machinery ("The task list was cut off…") showed up and vanished on its own. The only thing created was an empty "Work" project, which means the bakery shifts became a project instead of busy time, and the ED II deadline, the actual thing to prepare for, was nowhere on Today, Coming up or Draft plans. That breaks the profile's rules "abandons if the next action is not visible" and "unresolved if there is no final confirmation".
- **Highest emotional friction point:** Also Step 4, with the build-up in Steps 2–3. The clarify questions made Priya admit things that hurt ("Not picked yet", "Not started"). She paid that cost expecting a plan, and Tangent gave her nothing back. Admitting things and then getting no result turned mild discomfort into distrust and embarrassment.
- **Final decision:** Abandoned at Step 4 of a possible 15. She never reached "save the plan", "mark the first step done" or "tell Tangent how it went".
- **Emotional summary:** I went in tense. The page was full of someone else's stuff and the Canvas button wouldn't tell me what it reads, but the box was right there, so I wrote everything down: the essays, the rec, my shifts. When it quoted me back word for word, I relaxed a little. Okay, it's listening. Then it started asking things I didn't want to answer. No, I haven't picked a prompt. No, I haven't started. Every tap felt like confessing, and I kept wondering how many more there would be. I told myself it'd be worth it once I saw a plan that kept Wednesday nights and weekend mornings clear. Instead I watched "Placing…" for half a minute, read something about a task list being cut off that didn't mean anything to me, and then the message just disappeared. All that came out was an empty "Work" folder. My deadline was gone. I felt a bit stupid for opening up to it, and I don't trust it now. If I can't tell whether it saved anything, I'm not giving it my Canvas feed.
- **Tangent-specific checks:**
  - **Context (did Tangent use what she said?):** Only partly. Tangent kept her exact words (quoted back in Step 2) and asked relevant follow-ups about the essay. But the end result dropped the ED II deadline and turned the bakery shifts into an empty "Work" project instead of blocked time. She never saw evidence that her shifts would be respected, which was her main test.
  - **Different from generic apps (Todoist/Notion/ChatGPT):** The clarify step is what sets Tangent apart. It gave a reason ("so this fits you"), offered one-tap answers, and always showed Skip and Stop and plan. But because nothing came out at the end, this run ended up worse than a generic to-do app. Todoist would at least have saved "ED II due Jan 5" as a task.
  - **Follow-through (check-ins, round review, daily bubble):** Couldn't be evaluated. No plan existed, so there was no first step to mark done and no "how did it go" moment. The Notifications badge went to 6 unread, but nothing showed it was tied to her input.
  - **Trust (what is stored, can she control it?):** Weak. "Goes to: Auto" and Connect Canvas don't say where data goes or what is read. After placing, she couldn't tell what had been stored (was the deadline saved anywhere?), and the "Work" project appeared without explanation. Nothing she saw suggested she could check or control what was kept.
- **Detected risks:**
  - Real users who type a capture with two different things in it (a deadline plus recurring commitments) may silently lose the main item while the secondary one becomes an empty project.
  - When the planning step fails quietly, the user may think Tangent "did nothing" and give up after the first session. This is worst for anxious or perfectionist students who just admitted they're behind.
  - Internal status text ("The task list was cut off…") exposes system state that users can't act on, and because it disappears, users can't tell success from failure.
  - Clarify questions about three different essays don't say which one they're asking about, so answers may get applied to the wrong essay.
  - With no visible question count, people with sensitive or avoided tasks may tap Stop or quit midway.
  - Without data and connector transparency, careful users will never connect Canvas or Calendar. That limits how much Tangent can do to stand out.
- **Structural insight:** The capture → clarify → plan loop has a dead end. When planning degrades or fails, the flow just stops instead of falling back to something the user can see. Clarify works well enough to make the user invest, emotionally and in time. That raises the stakes, so a silent ending is worse here than a plain to-do app that just saves the task. Also, recurring commitments (shifts) and deadlines in the same capture don't seem to go to the right places: shifts should become busy time, and the deadline should get a plan.
- **Fix this first:** After clarify, Home must never end without a visible result. If placing can't produce a full plan, show a persistent card in place of the add box. Name the captured item ("ED II application, due Jan 5"), say plainly what happened ("I couldn't finish the plan"), and offer two buttons: "Try again" and "Save as task for now". Never let the status line disappear without leaving a result or an error behind.
