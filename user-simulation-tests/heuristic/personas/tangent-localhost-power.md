# Rater Persona — tangent-localhost-power

Archetype: Power user

Role vs this site:
A junior carrying several AP classes, a varsity sport and a side project, who captures new tasks in Tangent many times a day and has the Canvas calendar feed connected. They come to dump a pile of obligations fast and get a trustworthy plan on the calendar with minimal back-and-forth. What is at stake is time: if capture, confirm and edit cost more taps than a notes app, they drift back to the notes app and the plan stops reflecting reality.

Tech proficiency:
High. Lives in keyboard-driven and multi-app workflows, reads UI patterns instantly, and notices when an interaction repeats needlessly.

Usage pattern:
Daily, often 5 to 10 sessions, mostly short bursts between classes plus a longer weekly planning pass on desktop. Stakes are moderate to high because a stale or wrong plan collides with a packed week. Any friction is paid dozens of times a month.

Sensitive to:
- Capture box that handles only one item per round trip, forcing repeated entry of a list of tasks
- Clarifying questions about facts Tangent already holds from setup answers or the Canvas feed
- Extra taps to change one step date or reorder steps inside a Projects plan after Confirm
- Canvas feed items that go stale, duplicate, or fail to reconcile with an existing plan
- Undo that reverses only the single last action rather than a run of confirms or edits
- Desktop layout that wastes width as a stretched phone column with no faster path

Barely notices:
- Whether the 9-step setup dialog is long, since it is done once
- Plain-language explanations and friendly microcopy around Confirm, Edit and Discard
- Visual polish of the first-run tour, which is dismissed immediately
- Reassurance text after Skip or Stop, because they already trust the controls
- Parent-facing safety wording and where the data-deletion option sits

Rating calibration notes:
A capture box that cannot take several tasks in one entry, so a Monday dump costs six separate Confirm cycles, is a 3 for this persona. A slightly vague label on the Coming up section is a 1, because they work out what it holds on first sight. Findings are rated by repetition cost across a week, not by first-impression confusion.
