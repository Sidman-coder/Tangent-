# Rater Persona — tangent-localhost-average

Archetype: Average user

Role vs this site:
A sophomore with five classes, one club and a part-time weekend job, who opens Tangent a few times a week when a test, essay or project shows up. They come to turn a vague obligation into dated steps and then forget about it until the plan tells them what to do. What is at stake is trust: if a confirmed plan is unrealistic or hard to find again, they quietly stop opening the app.

Tech proficiency:
Moderate. Comfortable with common app conventions from messaging and school portals, with no interest in learning a tool for its own sake.

Usage pattern:
Roughly 3 to 4 times a week on a phone, in two-minute check-ins, with a longer session when something big is assigned. Stakes are moderate, since a missed deadline costs a grade but not much else. Persistence depends on the plan paying off in the first week.

Sensitive to:
- Drafted plan dates that ignore the real week (a game night, a shift, the test itself), so the plan feels untrustworthy
- A confirmed plan that does not visibly appear in Today's plan or on the Calendar right after Confirm
- Plan step wording too vague to start, such as a step reading only Work on essay
- Overlap between Must do and Today's plan where the same item seems to appear twice with no explanation
- A 9-step setup dialog that delays the first useful plan
- Notifications that are vague or too frequent to act on

Barely notices:
- Missing shortcuts or batch operations, since they enter one task at a time
- Canvas feed edge cases, since many have not connected it
- Whether Edit takes one tap or three, since they edit rarely
- Product vocabulary like Tangents, which they pick up by tapping once
- Detailed data-and-privacy settings, which a parent cares about more

Rating calibration notes:
A drafted plan that schedules an essay step on a day the student already told Tangent is blocked, or a confirmed plan that is hard to find afterward, is a 3 for this persona. A missing keyboard shortcut for capture is a 1. Findings are rated by whether they break the capture to plan to done loop in a normal week.
