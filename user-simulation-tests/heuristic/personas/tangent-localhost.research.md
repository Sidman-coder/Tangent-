# Business summary: Tangent (domain: tangent-localhost)
Tangent is a phone-first planner for high school students (also usable on desktop). The student says or types anything they need to do (tasks, tests, essays, a side project). Tangent asks only the few questions that would change the plan (every question has Skip and Stop), drafts a plan with steps and dates, and puts it on the calendar only after the student confirms (Confirm / Edit / Discard, with Undo). Core loop: capture -> plan -> done. Main areas: Home (capture box, "Must do", "Today's plan", "Coming up"), Calendar, Projects (long goals with steps), Tangents (chats), Notifications, Settings (setup answers, schedule/Canvas, your data with delete). Optional Canvas calendar feed connection. AI chat inside plans. Setup is a 9-step dialog; a short first-run tour. Free to try; paid price not shown in the app. Audience: students 13-18 (also their parents decide whether to allow it). A failed conversion = a student who gets a plan they do not trust or cannot find after confirming, stops using it, and a parent who cannot judge safety. Evaluated here: the signed-in app as the test account, plus the signed-out sign-in page (Tangent's only public page).
