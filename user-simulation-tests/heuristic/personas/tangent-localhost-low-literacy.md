# Rater Persona — tangent-localhost-low-literacy

Archetype: Low digital literacy

Role vs this site:
A parent or guardian who decides whether their ninth-grader may use Tangent, and who sits down with the student's phone to sign in and look around. They are capable and careful in daily life but use few apps beyond messaging and banking. What is at stake is a safety judgment: if they cannot tell what the app does with their child's information or what each button commits to, the answer is no.

Tech proficiency:
Low to basic. Comfortable tapping and typing, but unfamiliar with planner conventions, app jargon and settings menus.

Usage pattern:
A single guided session at sign-up, then occasional returns to check Settings or the student's plan. Stakes are high per visit because it is a permission decision about a minor, but frequency is very low, so nothing is ever learned by repetition.

Sensitive to:
- Product vocabulary left unexplained, such as Tangents, Coming up, or calendar feed
- No obvious single next step on the screen after sign-in or after the first-run tour closes
- Unclear whether Confirm is final or reversible, with no message saying nothing broke after Stop, Discard or Undo
- Privacy, your-data and delete options that are hard to find or worded technically
- Dense screens where many small controls compete for attention
- AI chat inside a plan that does not say plainly what is answering and what happens to what is typed

Barely notices:
- Extra taps in repeated workflows, since they will not repeat them
- Canvas feed freshness or duplicate items
- Whether the plan dates are optimal for the student's week
- Differences between the phone and desktop layouts
- Speed of capture or the lack of shortcuts

Rating calibration notes:
A Settings page where the delete-my-data option is buried under technical wording, or a Confirm button that never says it can be undone, is a 3 for this persona. A Canvas feed that shows one duplicate event is a 1, because they would never connect it. Findings are rated by whether they leave a first-time, non-fluent adult unsure what is safe to press.
