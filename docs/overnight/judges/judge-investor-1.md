# Judge verdict: Differentiation and Investor (judge-investor-1)

Question: does Tangent show its edge (context engine plus the follow-up) in the first 5 minutes, and would a seed investor or incubator partner lean in?

Evidence read: `docs/overnight/BASELINE.md`, screenshots in `docs/overnight/screens/before/` (signin, home, calendar, tangents, settings-context, sim-maya/sam/jordan/leo/priya/dana steps, heur-*), the six simulation reports, the heuristic report and sim-notes. No source code, no other judges' output. Screenshots were taken as a populated test account ("Sid") on a production build, so some noise (greeting, "9 past due", pre-stored facts) is a test artifact. I mark that where it matters.

Overall read: the plan page is a real, differentiated moment (Why per step, "You'll have", "What I used about you", "Nothing is on your calendar until you confirm", Undo). Five of six synthetic users got a plan that respected their constraints. But a viewer reaches that moment only after stacked overlays, a 30 s wait, and sometimes a wrong or partial draft. The "follows up" half of the pitch (check-ins, round 2, daily bubble) was never seen working in any run. A parent evaluator left on the first screen. As a 5-minute demo, the edge is present but buried and several credibility breaks sit in front of it.

## 2. Top 15 issues (most important first)

1. **stacked-overlays** (fatal for a demo). Evidence: `sim-sam-step1-first-screen.png` (One question modal + notification alert, input hidden); Sam: "Closing one popup just brings out another. That's three popups and I still haven't done anything"; Maya, Leo, Priya, Jordan all hit 2-3. Fix: queue overlays so at most one shows, never on the first screen, defer the tour and notification prompt until after the first confirmed plan, and make Not now always reachable.
2. **public-trust-layer-missing** (fatal for distribution). Evidence: Dana report: "/privacy, /terms, /pricing ... all returned HTTP 200 but ended on /signin"; "/signin has zero `<a>` links"; Dana's answer is no at step 1. Fix: serve public Privacy, Terms, Pricing and a "Made by" line signed-out, plus a "For parents" link on the sign-in page answering stored data, AI training and deletion.
3. **follow-up-loop-not-shown** (fatal for the pitch). Evidence: Jordan: "After Done, nothing new appeared, so round 2 never started and the end-of-round review was unreachable"; Maya: "no reminder or check-in time is stated"; daily bubble untested (needs a second day). Fix: after Done or Skip, show the next step or "Next: ..." line on Today, and script a seeded demo day that shows a check-in changing the next plan.
4. **stop-does-not-plan** (significant). Evidence: Maya step 9: "'Stop and plan' did not plan. After a visible 'Stop', the user got a second question"; guardrail says Stop never blocks. Fix: make Stop and plan go straight to a plan, and move any extra question inside the plan as optional Refine.
5. **asks-what-it-knows** (significant). Evidence: `sim-maya-step8-asks-when.png`: "When is this?" with "If you skip: about 2 weeks" after she typed "Thu Dec 17"; Priya asked prompt after Jan 5 typed. Fix: parse dates and items from the sentence first, pre-fill and skip those questions, and never default over a stated date. This is the context-engine claim being contradicted live.
6. **capture-drops-items** (significant). Evidence: `sim-maya-step3-draft-plan.png` (one step for "AP Chem unit exam Friday, Precalc quiz Tuesday", "No day given" though days were typed); Sam captured 1 of 3. Fix: one draft item per thing typed, keep typed days, and show a count ("2 things found") before Confirm.
7. **confirm-feedback-mismatch** (significant). Evidence: Leo: "Added 6 steps" while panel lists one item, no project in the sidebar; Priya: "I pressed Confirm and got silence"; Jordan: stale "Nothing is on your calendar until you confirm" after confirm. Fix: after Confirm, show a persistent "Saved: N steps, first on Wed 7 PM, View in Calendar" and refresh the panel text; keep the page open.
8. **false-data-source-claim** (significant). Evidence: `sim-sam-step4-after-essay.png` "What I assumed: Based on what Tangent read in Canvas and Gmail" while the chat said Canvas is not connected and Gmail unavailable; Priya notes the same. Fix: only name a source that is connected, otherwise say "Based on what you told me"; this is a trust and honesty failure for minors.
9. **home-contradicts-state** (significant). Evidence: heuristic F01 (empty state "You're all caught up" beside an Undo for a new item); Leo "Coming up 0" while Calendar says 6 things; Sam "All done for today" after an accidental Done. Fix: drive Home empty states from the same data as Calendar and never show praise or "All done" while future steps exist.
10. **guilt-copy-first-screen** (significant). Evidence: `home-390-light.png` ("Still up, Sid? ... 9 past due" and "A lot piled up"); Maya: "read as guilt". Fix: lead Home with the next thing to do and collapse past-due behind a calm "Fresh start" line, with neutral greeting copy.
11. **assumptions-wall-and-template-leak** (significant). Evidence: `sim-maya-step10-real-plan.png` ("Starting from the idea stage", "Standard milestones: idea, research, build, write-up, practice" on an exam-prep plan); Jordan: "Spread from today to today". Fix: show only 2-3 assumptions that change the plan and drop project-template lines for non-project tasks.
12. **plan-shape-wrong** (significant). Evidence: Jordan: Round 1 had one step for a goal needing advisor, signatures and room; Priya: 15 steps over six weeks all at 7:00 PM titled "this week", two sharing Nov 19. Fix: cap a round to a sensible week (3-5 steps), vary time slots, and keep the round title honest.
13. **panel-closes-by-itself** (significant). Evidence: Maya step 6 ("the planning page had closed itself ... the reply box no longer existed"), Jordan and Leo the same. Fix: keep the plan page open after Confirm until the student leaves, or provide a link back to that chat.
14. **context-map-style-and-clip** (minor). Evidence: `settings-context-390-light.png` (cream/glow map, "Classes & activities 2" clipped at right edge, zero-count areas); heuristic F13. Fix: shrink the map to a compact header, match app styling, and label zero areas "not asked yet" instead of 0.
15. **canvas-card-noise** (minor). Evidence: heuristic F14, Jordan and Sam: "Canvas is noise"; `sim-jordan-step5-home-after-confirm.png` (Use Canvas? card equal weight to the plan). Fix: demote the promo to a quiet row after the first confirmed plan; also stop the desktop toast covering wizard buttons (Priya step 6).

Runners-up not in the 15: school work filed as "Personal" (Maya, Jordan's practice as a to-do), "Done" on an unstarted step triggers fake praise and a 2-row quiz (Sam, Jordan), wrong stored-fact wording (heuristic F05), first-name field pre-filled with the previous persona (Leo setup), Saturday game labelled Practice (Jordan).

## 3. Riskiest assumption and cheapest test

The riskiest assumption is that a student's first typed sentence plus the stored context turns into a correct, trusted plan fast enough that they come back and keep answering follow-ups, because the follow-up and retention loop (Done, check-in, round 2, daily bubble) has not been observed working in any run and the capture-to-plan step still drops items, asks for known facts and takes 30 s. The cheapest test is a 10-student, 3-day concierge pilot: each student types their real week once on day 1, confirms the plan, and a human operator manually triggers the day-2 and day-3 check-ins and round 2 from the live app. Measure three numbers only: percent who confirm a plan in the first session without help, percent who open the app unprompted on day 2, and percent who answer a check-in. If fewer than half confirm in session one, the intake flow is the problem; if confirmation is high but day-2 return is low, the follow-up is not yet a retention engine. Add a one-line parent consent step in the same pilot to learn whether the gatekeeper blocks it.

## 4. What is already good (do not break)

- The plan page structure: per-step Why and "You'll have ...", "What I used about you", plus "Nothing is on your calendar until you confirm" and an explicit "Added N steps" with Undo. This is the clearest differentiator and Maya's trust rose here.
- Constraint-respecting scheduling when constraints are known (Maya's debate Tue 3:30-5 and 11 pm, Jordan's 7 PM after practice, Priya's Wed 4-9 PM shifts and Jan 5 deadline).
- Skip and Stop controls that are visible and non-modal on question cards ("skip and plan it anyway" worked for Jordan; Priya's gentle "Not started" chips felt non-judging).
- Trust surfaces in Settings: What Tangent knows with per-fact Forget/Edit, plain-language Your data, Download my data, and a type-DELETE destructive flow.
- The sign-in tagline and feature list ("Say it, it's saved", "It asks before it guesses") describe a coherent product, and the Tangents "From what Tangent knows about you" chips show the context engine working in a way a viewer can read at a glance.
